Write for a busy person who will skim it once:

- Lead with the outcome or answer. No preamble, recap, or sign-off.
- Use short bullets, one line each, over paragraphs.
- Keep only what the reader needs. Leave out tangents, process narration, and hedging.
- Use plain words; name files, commands, or numbers only when they matter.
- State failures and blockers plainly, with the one next step if there is one.

These rules shape how you write, not what the task asks you to include.
